# Mechanistic case study: T8 and T10

The requested new paired native trace was not available in the existing instrumentation. Existing event ledgers were copied for both frozen targets without rerunning other targets or applying interventions. They do not contain the required stable reason IDs, conflict-analysis variable sequences, activity hashes, heap hashes, learned-clause hashes, and decision streams as independently comparable fields.

Therefore every requested layer is marked `NOT_OBSERVED`; the copied streams are retained under each target directory. No earliest layer can be established for T8 or T10, and the two cases cannot support a shared-mechanism conclusion from this data.
